Parallelism — gpt-oss-120B, 4 GPUs in every arm

    

      

      
Matched aggregate concurrency. Above cc=2, four TP1 replicas beat one TP4 engine on throughput and latency simultaneously — no trade. Solo 8k prefill: TP1 125.2 ms, TP2 125.6 ms, TP4 75.5 ms, so TP2 buys nothing and TP4 recovers only 1.66× on 4× the silicon.

      
On the cached workload this reverses without prefix-affinity routing: DP4 round-robin drops to 81.75% hit and 304,369 tok/s against TP4's 467,657. With cache_aware it recovers to 90.75% and 582,715 — the best arm measured.

    

  


  

    Prefill isolated at OSL=1; TTFT is p50 unless noted. Cached-workload hit ceilings are tokenizer-dependent and each model was measured against its own realized ceiling rather than the nominal 93.75% — gpt-oss 93.73%, GLM 93.69%, Gemma 93.74%, Qwen 93.75%, Kimi 93.80%. Cached-workload throughput counts cache hits and runs ~16× inflated; true new-token rates are called out per model. GLM-5.2 W1 shows endpoints only. gpt-oss was re-measured end-to-end on one host and build after a tokenizer provenance gap; its earlier figures came from a different host and a base 15,476 commits apart, so cross-run deltas there are build-confounded.
